Indiana Medicaid Notebook

Clear, plain-language guidance: Who Qualifies, Who Accepts, Who Drives, and How to Apply.

About This Project

The Indiana Medicaid Notebook gives Hoosiers fast, direct answers about the state's Medicaid program. Core sections cover Who Qualifies?, Who Accepts?, Who Drives?, and How to Apply.

How It Was Built

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Mobile-first and accessible by design. No tracking, no accounts, no data collection.

FAQ

Q: Is this an official site?
A: No. It points to official Indiana sources but is an independent project.

Q: Does it store personal data?
A: No. It runs fully in your browser.
