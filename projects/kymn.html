Kentucky Medicaid Notebook

Clear, plain-language guidance: Who Qualifies, Who Accepts, Who Drives, and How to Apply.

About This Project

The Kentucky Medicaid Notebook is designed to give Kentuckians fast, direct answers. Core sections include Who Qualifies?, Who Accepts?, Who Drives?, and How to Apply.

How It Was Built

This build uses simple HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript for responsive navigation. Accessibility and mobile-first reading were priorities. No tracking, no accounts, no data collection.

FAQ

Q: Is this an official site?
A: No. It points to official Kentucky sources but is an independent project.

Q: Does it store personal data?
A: No. It runs fully in your browser.
