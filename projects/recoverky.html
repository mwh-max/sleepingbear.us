RecoverKY

Plain-language SNAP triage for Kentuckians — and a state-by-state rules guide for everyone.

About This Project

RecoverKY helps Kentuckians navigate the SNAP system when something goes wrong. It covers eligibility reviews, work requirements, case worker communication, benefit recovery and EBT card issues. A two-question triage gets users to the right section immediately. There is also a Social Worker view for advocates working on behalf of clients.

RecoverKY/Recipe

recoverky.health/recipe is a companion tool that surfaces state-specific SNAP rules — the quirks, exceptions, and little-known requirements that vary across states. Users select their state and get a plain-language breakdown of what applies to them.

How It Was Built

Built with plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No tracking, no accounts, no data stored. Policy reflects Kentucky SNAP rules as of April 2026.

FAQ

Q: Is this an official site?
A: No. It references official Kentucky sources but is an independent project. It does not replace kynect or a case worker.

Q: Does it store personal data?
A: No. Everything runs in your browser.
